BOUNDARIES
- UK GDPR breach notification is risk-based and is the organisation's duty.
  Internal staff reporting should be prompt. The 72-hour notification period runs
  from organisational awareness, not a permission for individual staff to wait.
- PECR timescale reform concerns public electronic communications providers.
  It is not presented as a universal duty for ordinary SME workers.
- The Cyber Security and Resilience (Network and Information Systems) Bill is
  treated as a Bill in the checked parliamentary record, not enacted law.
- FTC and Swiss NCSC sources explain technical/social-engineering points, not UK law.
- Recall/deletion promises are presented as evidence for assessment, not automatic
  proof of containment. Local response teams should document their decisions.
- VPN, security profile, password recovery and device-isolation examples defer
  to the organisation's approved procedures.
- No false claim of independent testing, accreditation, authorship or certification
  is made. The publisher credit identifies who publishes the work.
