WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.12

Clarified the different reporting duties for a business deciding why and how personal information is used and a business handling that information for a customer following the customer's instructions. The first assesses regulator reporting and its own 72-hour clock; the second tells the customer without unnecessary delay once aware. Added this to the manager legal detail, reading version, guide and source notes. Staff still report quickly.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.11

A single plain-English revision across the interactive course, all possible
task outcomes, modules, practice, help, manager card, reading version,
team guide, README, source notes and listing.

Specialist terms are removed or explained where needed. The approved
private-network connection (VPN) keeps its limits. Only approve sign-ins
you started. Password changes are kept separate from signing an account
out on devices or apps where it is already signed in.

The personal information guidance still covers wrong sharing, access,
loss, changes, damage and inability to use information. Staff report
quickly. The organisation assesses risk, records decisions and tells the
regulator or affected people when required. The 72 hours is not a staff
waiting period. Qualification, privacy and completion-record limits remain.

Module summaries now match the revised section headings. All choices,
possible outcomes, completion gates and saving behavior remain in place.
Supported public saved versions: 1.0.2 through 1.0.7, 1.0.9 and 1.0.10.
The held 1.0.8 test state is accepted safely, not called a public release.

RECENT EARLIER FIXES
1.0.10: reading source links fit narrow screens. Printing opens feedback
and restores the sections that were open before printing. With scripts
off, the reading version explains that sections must be opened manually.
1.0.9: saving pauses if another course tab changes stored progress. Use
one tab and reload the latest progress. This does not guarantee safe
simultaneous edits. No answers or results are automatically combined.

TESTING LIMITS
Computer-run checks and fictional learner walkthroughs are not real learner
trials. No measured learning, staff completion time, long-term memory,
formal accessibility approval or perfect-usability claim is made.
