WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.7

- Trying another route repeats report-building and AI inbox routing, while
  keeping earned completion. Replays no longer skip those hands-on actions.
- If saving fails during gameplay, a persistent warning explains that work
  remains on the page but recent progress may be lost on close/reload.
- If browser data cannot be removed during opt-out/reset, the course says so
  instead of claiming all saved data is gone. Current-page progress still
  resets. The warning tells shared-device users to clear the site through
  browser settings. A successful retry removes the warning.
- Valid v1.0.6 saved progress is retained alongside earlier supported versions.

Fresh labelled scripted/model personas cover repeat and returning learners,
busy/mobile workers whose storage fills, and privacy-conscious shared-device
users with blocked or silently ignored deletion. Existing and new tests
cover 108 edges/80 nodes, reporting/replays, state migration, failures,
assessment pass/fail, printing, contrast cases, keyboard focus and layout.
Automated accessibility checks found no violations on tested screens.
Save/clear failure warnings inspected on mobile and desktop.

No human/native-device/screen-reader, learning-retention, duration,
competence, accessibility-conformance or perfect-usability claim.
The guide is unchanged, five pages. No legal/accreditation claim changed.
