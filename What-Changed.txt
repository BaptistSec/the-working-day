WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.10

- Reading transcript source URLs wrap on narrow screens instead of pushing
  the page horizontally. Checked at 320, 390 and 1280 CSS pixels.
- Printing the transcript with scripts enabled opens feedback/details for
  the printout and restores the reader's prior open/closed sections after.
  Mixed open/closed states and repeated print events are handled.
- With scripts off, the reading route still works. A visible note explains
  that sections must be opened manually before browser printing; closed
  sections may not print. The Print button is labelled with feedback.
- Supported earlier saved progress is retained, including public v1.0.9.

Labelled scripted/model personas: scripts-disabled employee, assisted
reading facilitator, narrow-screen offline reader and reader printing mixed
open/closed sections. No-JS navigation, internal links, reflow, print event
restoration and PDF feedback content checks pass. Full prior regression,
108 edges/80 nodes and automated accessibility checks pass locally.
Actual source-list mobile, no-JS note and PDF pages inspected.

No human/native-device/screen-reader/all-browser-print, conformance,
learning-effectiveness, duration or perfection claim. The facilitator guide
is unchanged, five pages. No legal or accreditation content changed.
