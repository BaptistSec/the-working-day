WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.4

- Continue now takes you to the next unfinished task or module. When finished,
  it takes you to your learning record.
- Saving options tell you whether saving is on or unavailable. Inspected
  request details survive a refresh when saving is enabled.
- The first task includes a workspace setup check: device, connection,
  surroundings and a known support route.
- Modules show a short habit summary, with examples available on demand.
- Optional practice shows one inbox card at a time, with previous/next.
  Its report exercise and discussion prompts open only when wanted.

The next review used fresh model personas: a returning learner, an employee
with storage disabled, a first-day worker on a phone, a methodical reader,
a keyboard-only revisiting learner and a manager preparing a rollout.
These were scripted/model walkthroughs, not human feedback. The fixes address
specific confusion and duplicate content found during those checks.

Previous valid 1.0.2/1.0.3 saved progress is retained in the same browser.
Saving is optional, no data is sent, and the print-only name is not saved.
The guide remains five pages, with its local-saving description aligned.

All 108 branch edges and 80 reachable nodes remain. Automatic accessibility,
logic, storage and responsive checks pass locally. This is not proof of
perfect usability, real screen-reader support, validated duration or
workplace competence. No new legal or accreditation claims were added.
