WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.5

- Missed assessment answers are now shown one at a time, with the chosen
  answer, best answer, explanation and relevant learning module. Previous
  and Next keep review manageable. The full 16-answer review remains.
- Keyboard assessment navigation focuses the current question. Missing
  answers have an associated error; choosing an answer removes stale errors.
- Module feedback announcements include the actual explanation, not only
  a generic correct/try-again message.
- If browser saving fails when enabled, the options immediately show that
  saving is unavailable instead of claiming it is on. In-memory work remains.
- Valid saved 1.0.2/1.0.3/1.0.4 progress is retained under the existing key.

This round used labelled scripted/model personas: a novice with four misses,
an expert with no misses, a confident learner with one miss, a keyboard-only
learner, a mobile reader, a returning v1.0.4 learner and a learner whose
browser storage becomes full. These are not observed human testers.

All 108 branch edges, 80 reachable nodes, existing practice, pass/retake,
printing and corruption checks pass locally. Automated accessibility checks
found no violations on tested screens; this is not a conformance claim.
No real screen-reader/native-device trial, measured duration, retention,
workplace competence or perfection claim is made.

Research informed clearer corrective feedback, error recovery and supportive
reporting. Learning research does not establish that immediate feedback is
always best or validate this course. No legal or accreditation claim changed.
The facilitator guide is unchanged and remains five pages.
