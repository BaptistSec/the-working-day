WHAT CHANGED

The course is quieter, and there is more to do before choosing an answer:
- Inspect a sender, destination, caller or request before deciding.
- Put a routine inbox request on the right work route.
- Build a first incident report from facts, leaving guesses and passwords out.
- Take the assessment one question at a time, with a previous button.

Navigation has three links instead of six. Home has one Start/Continue action.
Old requests, decision trails and longer explanations open only when needed.
Outcomes are shorter. Repeated simulation labels and duplicate next buttons are gone.

Objective screen counts, at a fresh 390px-wide browser window:
- Home: 546 visible words before, 95 now.
- Payroll task entry: 220 before, 70 now.
- First module: 372 before, 190 now.
These counts exclude text behind closed disclosures. They are screen-density
measurements, not human readability or learning-performance results.

Two rounds of model-persona/scripted review covered a non-technical manager,
busy worker, less confident reader, keyboard-only user, mobile-only user,
technical skeptic, fast clicker and slow reader. Round two separated inspection
from deciding, removed the duplicate question after report building, and cut
a repeated chat preview. The automated checks passed after those fixes.

No human pilot, native-device test or real screen-reader test was performed.
This does not establish perfect usability, formal accessibility conformance,
validated duration, retention or workplace competence.

Valid saved progress from 1.0.2 is retained in the same browser. Earlier 1.0.1
progress is not migrated. Saving remains optional, and saves unfinished assessment answers and the
current question when enabled. No answers or reports are sent.
The printable reading version includes the new active exercises for assisted use.
The five-page facilitator guide is retained; it is not a new response plan.

Law and guidance currency remains 6 October 2026. No new legal claims are made.
