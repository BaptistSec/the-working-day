WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.20

One centered workspace replaces the simultaneous navigation rails. The task list starts closed and opens as its own view. The optional guide starts closed for new sessions; an explicitly saved guide preference is retained. Menu still provides all course tools.

Each task has one meaningful title. Learning and practice checks are separate views with a clear Continue action and a way back. Both remain in printed modules. Progress details are available when opened, rather than competing with the next step.

More space around text, tasks and choices, a readable text width, and a quieter menu. No teaching choices, assessment questions, completion rules, scores, price or saved-progress key changed. No human learner or completion-time claim.
